# User Guide — TechSelect

TechSelect is a job board for software, web and automation work. One account can be a
**developer** (looking for work), a **client** (hiring), or both; switch between them from the
profile menu.

## Getting started

1. **Sign up** with Google, GitHub or email, then choose how you will use it first. The other
   role can be added later in Settings → Roles.
2. **Onboarding** walks you through the essentials and saves as you go.
   - Developers: *About you* → *Skills & rates* → *Evidence* (a portfolio site, or at least two
     projects) → *Publish*.
   - Clients: *Your company* → *First brief* → *Publish*.

There is no approval queue: anyone can publish, and clients decide who fits.

## Developers

- **Profile.** Your headline, bio, skills, rates and availability, plus the work itself:
  portfolio projects with photos, links and outcomes, work experience, education and
  certifications. *Import from resume* can fill sections from a PDF; nothing is added until you
  choose what to keep.
- **Connected accounts.** Connect GitHub and LinkedIn from your profile, so the links on it are
  accounts you have proved you own rather than typed URLs. You can disconnect either at any time.
- **Publishing.** A draft is visible only to you. Publishing makes the profile public and lets it
  be matched; you can unpublish at any time.
- **Matches.** Jobs ranked by how well your work fits, each with a one-sentence reason. Matching
  reads what you write about your projects, so specific descriptions and outcomes match better.
- **Applying.** Apply with a short note. Track each application's status: *Applied*, *Viewed*,
  *Shortlisted*, *Hired* or *Not taken forward*. You can withdraw at any time.
- **Saved jobs.** Private to you.

## Clients

- **Company profile.** The name, website and description shown on every job you post.
- **Jobs.** Write a brief (description, required and nice-to-have skills, budget and engagement
  type). Save it as a draft, publish it, and later close it, mark it filled, reopen or duplicate
  it.
- **Candidates.** Each job shows its applicants in order of fit, with a reason for each, plus
  suggested developers who fit but have not applied. You see an order and a reason, never a
  score on a person.
- **Shortlisting.** Move applicants through *Viewed*, *Shortlisted*, *Hired* or *Not taken
  forward*, and message the people you want to talk to.
- **Saved developers.** Private to you.

## Messages

Conversations are asynchronous and plain text, between the two people only. If someone is not
welcome, **block** them: neither of you can message the other, and they are not told. **Report**
a message, profile or job and a moderator reviews it. Developers can turn off new messages in
their profile settings.

## Settings and your data

- **Roles:** add the other role or switch between them.
- **Theme:** system, light or dark.
- **Export:** download everything you have entered as JSON.
- **Delete account:** removes your profile and content immediately. Conversations remain for the
  other person, with your side removed. See [PRIVACY.md](./PRIVACY.md).

## Fees

TechSelect charges no fees and handles no payments. Terms, rates and contracts are agreed
directly between client and developer.
